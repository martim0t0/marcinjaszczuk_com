## Why an interactive resume

A traditional PDF resume doesn't leave much room for personality or depth. Progressive disclosure via accordions lets a visitor see a quick summary first and dig into detail only where they're interested.

<Callout type="info">
  "I wanted this to run from a headless CMS that doesn't need web hosting."
</Callout>

The interactive part is more fun and interesting than a PDF or Word file, certainly better than paper. My favorite part about it is that you can get here by scanning a QR code.

Another reason for building this is because I wanted to experiment with a Headless CMS. What better place than on my resume? I can create a professional blog here detailing what I am working on and showcasing my projects, right inside my resume!
